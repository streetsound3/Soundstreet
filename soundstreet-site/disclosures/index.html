Disclosures

Last updated October 22, 2026

Who we are

Sound Street Research is an independent publication written by college students. We are not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. Sound Street is not affiliated with or endorsed by any college or university.

Not investment advice

Everything we publish is general market commentary for informational and educational purposes. It does not take into account any reader's financial situation, objectives or needs, and it is not personalized investment advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making investment decisions.

No offer or solicitation

Nothing on this site is an offer or solicitation to buy or sell any security. Companies named in our research are examples used to explain how an event affects an industry.

Holdings

Authors may hold positions in securities discussed in our research. Each note states whether its authors hold any of the securities it names.

Open calls

The calls on our Open Calls page are views we grade publicly to show how our research holds up. They are not a record of investment performance, and no portfolio follows them.

For advisors and firms

Advisor editions and client-ready updates are general commentary for financial professionals. Firms are responsible for reviewing any content, including through their own compliance process, before sharing it with clients. We do not see client data and do not communicate with a firm's clients.

Sources and accuracy

We cite the sources for the figures we publish. Market data and company figures can change, and sources can contain errors, so we cannot guarantee that every figure is complete or current.
