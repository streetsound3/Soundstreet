Open calls

Every call we make is dated, public and graded on a date we set in advance. Graded calls stay on this page, right or wrong.

How every call works

  1. 1 We state the call In one sentence, published with the note that argues it.
  2. 2 We set the grading date Before we know the answer, usually tied to a central-bank decision.
  3. 3 We say what proves us wrong A specific, checkable condition, not a hedge.
  4. 4 We grade it, and keep it here Right, wrong or mixed, with a short note on what we learned.
