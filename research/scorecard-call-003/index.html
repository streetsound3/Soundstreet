Call #003 graded: Brazil’s central bank held, as Alex called

November 7, 2026 · Michael Lawton · Grades Alex Uzzo’s November 2 note

GRADE

Right

The Central Bank of Brazil held the Selic at 13.75% on November 4, 2026.

The call

On November 2, Alex Uzzo called that the Central Bank of Brazil would hold the Selic at 13.75% on November 4. His reason: Lula’s re-election raised expectations of more government spending, bigger deficits and inflation risk. That would make the central bank pause instead of cutting again.

What happened

On November 4, the Copom voted unanimously to keep the Selic at 13.75%. That ended five straight quarter-point cuts this year (March, April, June, August and September), which had brought the rate down from 15%. The bank pointed to inflation expectations, tighter global conditions and an unclear fiscal path. It also projected higher inflation in the coming years and gave no signal on its next move.

Grade: Right

We said we would grade the call against the Copom’s statement on November 4, and the statement confirmed the hold.

What we’d note

The hold was widely expected by the day of the decision. Prediction markets priced it at about 89%. Alex made the call on November 2. That was less than a week after the election sell-off, when the IBOVESPA had fallen 13.88% in one day and the real about 8%. The day after the decision, the real was steady near R$5.45 per dollar, and the IBOVESPA rose about 0.8% in early trading.
