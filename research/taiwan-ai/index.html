FLASH NOTE · EMERGING MARKETS · TAIWAN

AI demand makes Taiwan the strongest emerging market this year

November 3, 2026 · Vincent Palatiello

Summary. Taiwan has officially surpassed China to become the largest constituent market in the MSCI Emerging Markets Index. Taiwan commands a weight of 28.94%, driven heavily by artificial intelligence and chip manufacturing. We believe this dominance will remain strong if semiconductor demand outpaces supply, primarily through Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC).

Our call: Maintain an overweight stance on Taiwanese equities through the end of 2027. Tracked as Call #004.

What happened

  • Taiwan surpassed China to become the largest constituent market in the MSCI Emerging Markets Index, with a weight of 28.94%.
  • The primary driver is the Information Technology sector, making up over 43% of the index.
  • The main company, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), accounts for 15.63% of the MSCI Emerging Markets Index.
  • Between April 11th, 2025, and April 11th, 2026, TSMC rose a staggering 153% in share price. Since then, the stock has continued to steadily climb and currently sits at about US$406 per share for its U.S.-listed ADR.

Why it happened

The surge in demand for semiconductors has led to high-volume orders. This gives companies like TSMC pricing power, allowing them to expand profit margins.

How it flows through

With Taiwan leading the MSCI Emerging Markets Index, this shift brings clear benefits to companies. Heavy capital spending by chipmakers leads to high operating leverage as factory utilization increases. In addition, capital inflows into equities improve domestic liquidity. These benefits outweigh any negative effects from currency fluctuations, especially when export earnings are brought back into the country. Still, heavy foreign outflows in June 2026 showed the market can swing sharply. The long-term outlook remains positive as global artificial intelligence demand continues to grow. That keeps earnings strong and supports our overweight stance on the market.

Names to watch

TSMC 2330 · TSM

▲ Market cap of NT$61.8 trillion

TSMC is the largest manufacturer of advanced semiconductor chips for major tech companies like Nvidia and Apple. Since its 1997 IPO, TSMC stock has risen an average of 18.7% a year. An initial investment of $1,000 in 1997 would now be worth around $133,500 in stock.

MediaTek 2454

▲ Market cap of NT$6.37 trillion

MediaTek is one of the world’s largest fabless chip designers, meaning it designs chips and has foundries like TSMC manufacture them. A share of MediaTek currently trades just under NT$4,000. MediaTek has gained major attention following a strategic $3.5 billion investment and collaboration commitment from Nvidia alongside Alphabet-backed initiatives.

What would prove us wrong

Our overweight stance on Taiwanese equities would be invalidated if:

  • Artificial intelligence capital spending declines by over 15% quarter over quarter.
  • TSMC’s factory utilization rate falls below 80% in any quarter of 2027.
  • Net foreign capital outflows exceed $10 billion in a single quarter.

The most serious threat to Taiwan is an invasion by China. The Chinese Communist Party (CCP) sees Taiwan as a breakaway province that needs to be reunified with the mainland. President Xi Jinping has tied this goal to China’s “national rejuvenation” target of 2049, though Beijing has not set a firm deadline.

Our call: Maintain an overweight stance on Taiwanese equities through the end of 2027. We track it as Call #004: the MSCI Taiwan Index beats the MSCI Emerging Markets Index from November 3, 2026 to December 31, 2027. We’ll grade it on MSCI’s published index returns.
