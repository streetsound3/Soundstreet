Call #007 graded: CoreWeave beat, and the stock jumped

November 11, 2026 · Michael Lawton · Grades our November 10 CoreWeave preview

GRADE

Wrong

CoreWeave closed at $60.77 on November 11, 2026, up 24.7% from $48.73 on November 10.

The call

On November 10, we called that CoreWeave (CRWV) would close lower on November 11 than on November 10. Our reason: given the growing disbelief in AI and tech, along with the ongoing Nvidia investigation, CoreWeave’s earnings release would be poor.

What happened

CoreWeave reported its third quarter after the close on November 10 and beat its own guidance on every line we watched. It also raised its full-year outlook.

Q3 2026 Our estimate Guidance Actual
Revenue$3.53B$3.45–3.60B$3.66B
Adjusted operating income$230M$200–260M$379M
Interest expense$900M$860–940M$846M
Revenue backlogAbout $125BNot guidedAbout $132.5B
Full-year 2026 revenue guidance—$12.4–13.2BRaised to $13.0–13.3B

The stock closed up 24.7% on November 11, and Nebius was up about 16% by mid-morning.

Grade: Wrong

We said we would grade the call against Nasdaq’s official closing prices. CoreWeave closed at $60.77 on November 11, above its $48.73 close on November 10.

Our own warning signs

Both of the note’s “what would prove us wrong” tests triggered on the night of November 10, before the stock moved: revenue came in above $3.60B, and backlog came in above $125B.

What we got wrong

We said interest was running about four times operating income. Operating income came in about 46% above the top of guidance and interest came in under it, so interest fell to about 2.2 times adjusted operating income, from 5.0 times in Q2.
